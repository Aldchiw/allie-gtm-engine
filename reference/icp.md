# Allie AI — ICP (demo)
_Status: illustrative, built from public info (Sep 2026). To be validated with Allie._

## ICP
An account qualifies only if ALL are true:
- company_type is manufacturer-continuous, manufacturer-discrete or manufacturer-hybrid (operates its own physical plants).
- Geography: Mexico, United States or LatAm.
- Size: 200+ employees, or at least 1 plant with multiple production lines.
- Pain: downtime, OEE, scrap or energy waste matter to the business; has a maintenance or continuous improvement function.

company_type labels (every account gets one): manufacturer-continuous, manufacturer-discrete, manufacturer-hybrid, distributor, system-integrator, machine-builder, software-vendor, competitor, wrong-industry, unknown.

Priority verticals: food & beverage, building materials (cement, steel, glass), CPG / personal care.
Secondary verticals: auto parts (Tier-1/Tier-2), metalworking and foundries, packaging, pharma / medical devices.

## Segmentation matrix
Axis 1 — process type: Continuous / Discrete / Hybrid.
Axis 2 — deployment archetype: Enterprise multi-plant / Mid-market (1-3 plants) / Greenfield-nearshoring.

| | Enterprise multi-plant | Mid-market 1-3 plants | Greenfield / nearshoring |
|---|---|---|---|
| Continuous | P1 — land-and-expand | P2 — standard motion | P2 — timing play |
| Discrete | P2 — throughput + scrap | P2 — standard motion | P1 — timing play |
| Hybrid | P2 | P3 | P3 |

Motion by archetype: enterprise = land one plant, expand to the corporate account; mid-market = standard sales cycle; greenfield = get in before the MES/SCADA stack is chosen.

## Signal sources
An account enters the pipeline only with a signal that has a source URL and a date.
1. Capacity expansion: new plant, new production line, nearshoring announcement. Freshness: 180 days.
2. Job postings: continuous improvement, reliability engineer, maintenance manager, OEE, Industry 4.0, plant digitalization. Freshness: 45 days.
3. Leadership change: new VP Operations, Plant Director or COO. Freshness: 180 days.
4. Systems change: ERP / MES / SAP migration or go-live. Freshness: 180 days.
5. Ownership or funding: new private equity owner or recent funding (efficiency pressure). Freshness: 365 days.

## Buying committee
Max 2 contacts per account.
- Champion (feels the pain): Maintenance Manager / Gerente de Mantenimiento, Reliability Engineer / Ingeniero de Confiabilidad, Continuous Improvement Manager / Gerente de Mejora Continua, Production Manager / Gerente de Producción.
- Economic buyer (signs): Plant Director / Director de Planta, VP Operations / Director de Operaciones, COO.
- Blocker to anticipate: IT/OT or cybersecurity (machine connectivity), Finance.
- Greenfield exception: Launch / Startup Manager is the champion; the Plant Director named for the launch is the buyer.
- Never lead with CIO or CEO.

## Messaging guardrails
- 6-8 lines. The signal goes in the first line.
- Speak the plant's language: OEE, unplanned downtime, scrap, energy waste.
- Outcome claims ONLY as Allie's publicly reported ranges (15-20% less unplanned downtime, 10-15 OEE points, 5-10% less material and energy waste), always framed as "in comparable plants". Never invent new numbers.
- Zero customer names in outreach unless Allie authorizes them.
- Spanish for Mexico/LatAm accounts, English for US accounts.
- Nothing that sounds AI-written. No hype words.
- Anti-burn: P1 accounts never get an untested angle; test new angles on P2/P3 first.

## Exclusions
- Distributors and resellers (no plant, no downtime to fix).
- System integrators and automation vendors (potential partners, not buyers — route to partnerships, not outbound).
- Machine builders / OEMs of equipment (partner channel, not direct buyer).
- Pure software companies.
- Competitors: MachineMetrics, and any other manufacturing AI / predictive maintenance vendor.
- Fewer than 50 employees.
- Outside Mexico, US and LatAm.
